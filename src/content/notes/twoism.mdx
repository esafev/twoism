This template is named after Twoism, a 1995 EP by Boards of Canada, the Scottish duo who took their name from the National Film Board of Canada documentaries they watched as kids.

Notes live in `src/content/notes`, one `.md` or `.mdx` file each. The home page lists them by year, newest first.

Create a new one with:

```sh
bun run note:create "A new title" "A short description"
```

It starts as a draft: visible in `bun run dev`, left out of the build and the RSS feed. Set `published: true` when it's ready.
